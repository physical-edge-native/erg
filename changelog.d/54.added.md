Leg firmware for the ESP32-S3 (firmware/leg-esp32): servo bus, IMU, foot switch, gait clock and base gait, Dyne's mlp-actor policy and timing for one leg on the bench; its hardware-free parts are tested on the PC bit for bit, the base gait's trigonometry uses arithmetic-only functions shared with training (erg/exactmath.py), and the firmware is built in CI (#54).
