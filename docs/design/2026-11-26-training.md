# Training: PPO on the CPU, the reward, and the policy sizes

Date: 2026-11-26. Issue: #42. Decided by the maintainer; this record was written with an AI assistant.
Written before any full training run.

## Decided (2026-11-26)

| Point | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Method | PPO (proximal policy optimization), written in erg (`erg/ppo.py`), PyTorch on the CPU, one thread per run | The usual method for learned legged locomotion. No new dependency; deterministic runs; the per-leg form (four legs sharing one policy) is simple to express. A library (Stable-Baselines3 and others) was the other option |
| Actor sizes | ReLU, 48 × 48 for both forms: 5,575 parameters per leg (59 inputs, 7 outputs), 6,204 central (67 inputs, 12 outputs) | Bit-exact in Dyne's WebAssembly runtime, and under the 8,192-parameter limit of Dyne's full `mlp-actor`. The first proposal (64 × 64 per leg, 128 × 128 central) exceeded that limit (8,455 and 26,764). No size fits the 2,048 limit of the small variant with 59 inputs |
| Where to train | Long runs on the maintainer's PC (22 threads), several runs at once as separate processes; short runs in the cloud session | One run uses one thread; the comparison needs many runs (forms × seeds) |

## Design choices made with the implementation (to be reviewed by the maintainer)

- **The same critic for both forms**: a value network (ReLU 128 × 128) on the central observation (every leg's
  sensors and the command), used only during training, never on the robot. The per-leg actors share one reward,
  and a value estimated from one leg's view alone would be poor; giving both forms the same critic keeps the
  comparison about the actors (the controllers that run on the robot).
- **Per-leg actions** are the 3 joint targets and the 4 learned message values of each leg; the four legs' log
  probabilities are summed into one joint action of the robot, so PPO's ratio is the robot's, as in the central form.
- **Messages during training** are passed directly (`Env(wire=False)`), which gives the same observations bit for
  bit as the wire format (#35) and is about three times faster.

## Reward (`erg/reward.py`), the same for both forms

Per control step (20 ms):

| Term | Weight | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Forward and sideways speed tracking | 1.0 | exp(-\|v_xy - c_xy\|² / 0.25²) with v in the torso's heading frame (m/s); width 0.1 before #49 |
| Turning speed tracking | 0.5 | exp(-(ω_z - c_turn)² / 0.5²) (rad/s); width 0.3 before #49 |
| Effort | -0.05 | mean over joints of (torque / stall torque)² |
| Smooth commands | -0.01 | mean over joints of (joint target change)² (rad²) |
| Steps (added in #49) | 0.5 | for each foot touching down: (time in the air − 0.15 s), only while moving is commanded (speed command above 0.05 m/s or turning above 0.1 rad/s) |
| Fall | -2.0 | once, and the episode ends |

The tracking widths were 0.1 m/s and 0.3 rad/s in the first version and are 0.25 m/s and 0.5 rad/s since #49
(see "Pilot runs" below). Since #49, commands are drawn in the full ranges scaled by a factor that grows linearly
from 0.3 to 1 over the first 30 % of the training steps.

A fall: torso below 0.12 m (standing: about 0.22 m) or tilted more than 45°. Episodes last 10 s (500 steps) at
most; each starts from the standing posture with a command drawn from `erg.env.COMMAND_MAX`.

## PPO settings

Joint target = standing posture + 0.5 rad × action. Gaussian actions with a learned state-independent standard
deviation (initial log std -1); the robot runs the mean. 8 environments per run, 128 steps per environment
between updates, 4 epochs of 4 minibatches, discount 0.99, GAE λ 0.95, clip 0.2, learning rate 3e-4, value
coefficient 0.5, gradient norm clipped at 1.0, no entropy bonus.

## Measured time (cloud session, one thread, 2026-11-26)

| Form | Environment step | Training update, per step | Total per step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per-leg (neighbour links, messages passed directly) | 812 µs | 297 µs | about 1.1 ms |
| Central | 540 µs | 267 µs | about 0.8 ms |

At about 1 ms per step, 10 million steps take about 3 hours per run on one thread. To be measured again on the
maintainer's PC, where several runs go in parallel.

## Pilot runs

### Pilot 1 (2026-11-26): first reward, per-leg form

Neighbour links, no loss or delay, seed 0, 2,000,896 steps, 8 environments; 19 minutes in the cloud session
(0.51 ms per environment step and 0.06 ms of update per step with 8 environments).

| Steps | Mean reward per step | Falls per 1,024 steps |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.16 | 7.9 |
| 200,000 | 0.30 | 2.1 |
| 1,000,000 | 0.43 | 2.0 |
| 2,000,000 | 0.53 | 2.1 |

In MuJoCo, the learned policy (mean action) did not fall in 10 s for any command, but it **did not walk**: for
forward 0.1, 0.15 and 0.23 m/s, backward 0.1 m/s, sideways 0.1 m/s and turning 0.5 rad/s, it stayed in place
(about 0.01 m/s; 3 seeds each). Standing still was a good local optimum of the first reward: the speed term
(width 0.1 m/s) paid almost nothing until the speed was close to the command, the turning term paid for standing
when the turning command was near zero, and the fall penalty discouraged trying. Decided by the maintainer
(#49): widen the tracking widths, add the step (air-time) term and the command curriculum, and run again.
