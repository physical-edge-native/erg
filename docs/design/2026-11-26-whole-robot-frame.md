# Frame of the whole robot: torso, right legs and foot switch

Date: 2026-11-26. Issue: #53. Decided by the maintainer where stated; the design choices below were made with the
implementation by an AI assistant and are to be reviewed by the maintainer. This record was written with an AI
assistant.

The parts are generated by `erg/torso.py` (torso) and `erg/frame.py` (legs, foot switch pocket), both with
build123d. `scripts/export_frame.py` writes STEP and STL files for printing. `tests/test_torso.py` checks the
torso, the right legs' parts, the foot switch pocket and their agreement with the MuJoCo model.

## Decided (2026-11-26)

| Point | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Torso, right legs' parts and foot switch mount in one issue | All are needed before the whole robot is printed (planned for 2026-12-05 to 12-12) and all are in the frame code |

## Design choices made with the implementation (to be reviewed)

| Point | Choice | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Torso layout | A flat deck, 3 mm thick, 35.2 mm above the abduction shafts, joining the end caps of the four abduction servos, which stand upright over their shafts as on the bench stand | Each leg is held exactly as on the bench, where it is tested first. The first idea, servos lying across the robot with a spine between them, failed the interference check: the hip yoke's base sweeps into the servo's end cap when the leg abducts inwards (71.5 mm^3 of overlap at -0.5 rad) |
| Battery | Under the deck between the legs, held by a 20 mm hook-and-loop strap through four slots; space for up to 110 x 50 x 30 mm | Keeps the centre of mass low and the battery removable; the exact battery model is not chosen yet |
| Electronics | Each leg's microcontroller, 5 V regulator and servo bus adapter on the deck, inwards of its hip (taped or tied); a cable hole next to each servo | Short wires to each leg's servos; the per-leg grouping follows the split control (one microcontroller per leg) |
| IMUs | Each in a 0.6 mm recess in the deck's top face right above its hip, glued or taped | Fixed to the torso; the deck's top face stays flat for printing it face down. The model's IMU sites moved from the hips' centres (an estimate) to the board's top face, 39.2 mm above the shafts |
| Right legs | Mirror images of the left legs' printed parts | The STS3215 is symmetric across its own xz plane, so a mirrored servo is the same servo turned half a turn about its long axis; no other part needs to change |
| Foot switch | Omron SS-5GLD in a pocket inside the foot, its actuator 2 mm inside the sole, the lever out through a slot in the sole; slid in from the outer side and glued; its wires through a 3 mm channel along the shank | The foot stays the 12 mm sphere of the model; the switch closes when the foot carries weight |

## Values to check before printing

- **The switch's size** (12.8 x 5.8 x 6.5 mm, from the Omron SS-5 series datasheet) and how far its lever stands
  out: test-fit one printed shank before printing all four, as for the servo dimensions.
- **The printer's bed**: the torso is 218 x 126 x 17 mm. The tests assume a 250 mm bed for the Bambu Lab X2D; to be
  checked against its build volume.
- **The battery's size**: the deck leaves room for up to 110 x 50 x 30 mm under it.

## Resulting masses, and changes to the MuJoCo model

| | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Torso | 0.75 kg (an estimate) | 0.547 kg: printed deck 96.8 g, four abduction servos 220 g, battery 0.13 kg, electronics 4 x 0.015 kg, wiring 0.04 kg (the last three are estimates, to be weighed after building) |
| Torso centre of mass | on the abduction shafts' height | 23.6 mm above them |
| Shank | 45.4 g | 44.8 g with the switch (pocket -1.6 g, switch about 1 g, an estimate) |
| IMU sites | at the hips' centres | on each IMU board's top face, 39.2 mm above the shafts |
| Torso collision box | 220 x 120 x 50 mm, centred on the shafts | 216.8 x 124.8 x 48.4 mm, from 10.2 mm below to 38.2 mm above the shafts |
| Total | 1.61 kg | 1.40 kg |

The standing posture and the trot gait of #32 still keep the robot up in MuJoCo (`tests/test_robot_model.py`,
`tests/test_gait.py`).

Printed parts for the whole robot (PLA, 100 % infill): torso 96.8 g once; left legs (hip 27.1 g, thigh 32.4 g,
shank 43.8 g) twice; right legs (the same masses) twice: about 510 g of filament, plus the bench stand (22.7 g) for
the first leg.

How to print the torso: its top face on the bed, the end caps pointing up.

Not checked: legs against each other (front and hind feet could meet when the legs reach far towards each other;
the MuJoCo model does not let robot parts collide either).
